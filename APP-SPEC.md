# Color Shift for iPhone: app spec

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-04 through a one-question-at-a-time interview. Items marked **(assumed)** were proposed by Claude, not chosen by the owner. Confirm them before they're built.

Visual details live in [COLOR-SHIFT-STYLE-GUIDE.md](COLOR-SHIFT-STYLE-GUIDE.md). The starting prompt for a build session is [first-prompt.md](first-prompt.md).

---

## Build

A native SwiftUI iPhone app that adapts **Matt D. Smith's Color Shift**, a web tool for choosing a background and text color that read well together. The original is at <https://github.com/mds/color-shift> and live at <https://labs.shiftnudge.com/color-shift>. The owner has Matt's permission.

It's a **portfolio piece**, shown through screen recordings and a written case study, and run from Xcode on the owner's own iPhone. The direction is **Matt's look, iPhone's feel**: his layout, type and signature animations, with Liquid Glass controls, native gestures and haptics.

It's built in three stages, each usable on its own:

1. **Version 1: colors and score.**
   - Opens on a random harmonious color pair.
   - Pull down for a new pair.
   - Edit either color with sliders and see the WCAG score live.
   - No photos and no network.
2. **Version 2: photos.**
   - Unsplash photos from the owner's own Vercel function.
   - Swipe between photos.
   - Pull down for a fresh photo.
   - Each photo supplies the color pair.
3. **Version 3: finish.**
   - App icon and an info screen crediting Matt.
   - A polish pass.
   - The owner records the demo videos.

## Content

**On screen, top to bottom (portrait):**

| Area | Shows |
|---|---|
| Top glass bar | The contrast score: grade (AAA / AA / AA Large / Fail) and ratio (e.g. `4.52:1`) |
| Color half | The background color filling the area, with a large **"Aa"** in the text color. Tapping toggles "Aa" ↔ a solid circle of the text color. **Confirm:** Matt removed this toggle from his live app on 2026-05-20 (commit `56402dc`, where tap became "swap colors"). We'd port it from his earlier version (commit `09875b0`) |
| Photo half *(v2)* | The current Unsplash photo, with "Photo by *Name* on Unsplash" in small type |
| Bottom glass bar | Two swatch buttons, text color and background color, each labelled with its hex value. Tapping one opens that color's sliders above the bar; tapping it again closes them |
| Slider panel | Tabs **OKLCH / HSB / RGB** and three sliders. Each has a live gradient track, a label (`L C H`, `H S B` or `R G B`) and a value |
| Info screen *(v3)* | "An iOS adaptation of Color Shift by Matt D. Smith", with links to the original and to Unsplash |

**Text in the app:**
- "Aa" (fixed, not editable);
- hex values in capitals without `#` (e.g. `1A1A2E`);
- grades and ratios exactly as Matt's site prints them;
- slider labels and values;
- photo credits;
- the info screen.

There are no onboarding screens, tutorials or empty-state illustrations.

**Version 1 layout (assumed):** with no photo yet, the color half fills all the space between the two bars.

## Constraints

- **Platform:** iPhone only, **portrait only**, **iOS 26+**. Built with Xcode 27, Swift 6 and SwiftUI. Develop in the iPhone 18 Pro simulator; the owner runs it on their own iPhone.
- **Repo:** `fernandezcarolina/color-shift` (public). Matt's web app is cloned read-only at `~/Desktop/GitHub/mds-color-shift` as the reference. Never edit it.
- **Color math must match Matt's exactly.** For any two colors, the app shows the same hex conversions, slider values, WCAG ratio and grade as `src/lib/color-engine.ts` and the slider helpers in `src/components/color-shift.tsx`.
  - Tests compare against reference answers generated by running his engine once.
  - The owner must allow that step, because sessions block running his code by default.
- **The random pair** comes from a port of Matt's `generateRandomPair()`: a random harmony type (complementary, analogous, triadic, split-complementary or random) and his lightness and chroma ranges.
- **Unsplash (v2):**
  - **The key:** the access key lives only in the owner's Vercel function, as an environment variable. Never put it in the app, the repo or logs.
  - **Rate limit:** the function follows Matt's `/api/photos` route: random photos across his 44-word query pool, landscape orientation, and `?id=` lookup. The Demo limit is 50 requests/hour, which is fine for one person.
  - **Credits:** follow Unsplash's API rules. Photographer and Unsplash links get `utm_source` / `utm_medium`. Images are shown only from the URLs the API returns.
- **Offline (v2, assumed):** with no connection, the app keeps working on random color pairs and shows a quiet "offline" note in the photo half.
- **Distribution:** none. No App Store, no TestFlight, no paid Apple Developer account. Running from Xcode on the owner's phone with a free Apple ID is enough.
- **Credit:** named **Color Shift**, credited to Matt in the app (v3) and in the case study.

## Style

The full guide is [COLOR-SHIFT-STYLE-GUIDE.md](COLOR-SHIFT-STYLE-GUIDE.md). In short:

- **Matt's identity:**
  - near-black chrome (`#0f0e0f`);
  - Input Mono for every UI label;
  - two text grays, `#a39f9f` at rest and `#e5e0e0` selected;
  - 4 pt corners;
  - a 12 pt color swatch inside the hex buttons;
  - Instrument Serif for the "Aa".
- **iOS layer:** the top and bottom bars are **Liquid Glass** floating over the color and photo. Nothing else is glass.
- **Signature motion kept:**
  - rolling letters when labels change;
  - the "Aa" ↔ circle toggle;
  - color crossfades;
  - the slider grip that swells into a soft blurred dot while dragging.

## Behavior

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Launch (v1) | Random harmonious pair; the color eases in |
| Launch (v2) | First photo loads and its colors ease in; until then, a random pair |
| Pull down on the color/photo area | v1: new random pair. v2: fetch a fresh random photo and show it |
| Swipe left/right on the photo (v2) | The photo follows the finger like a filmstrip. Let go after dragging at least 20% of the width (Matt's rule) to move to the next/previous photo, wrapping around. Otherwise it springs back. The colors blend toward the incoming photo's pair during the drag |
| Tap text-color or background swatch | Opens the slider panel for that color, which pushes the halves up. Tap the same swatch to close it, or the other to switch |
| Switch OKLCH / HSB / RGB tab | Same color, new sliders. Grips and gradients glide to their new positions (0.2 s) |
| Drag a slider | The color updates live, and the grip swells. In OKLCH the chroma slider stays within the colors the screen can show (Matt's `maxChroma`) |
| Tap the color half | Toggles "Aa" ↔ solid circle |
| Tap the score | Nothing **(assumed)**. There's no APCA and no threshold list to open |
| Tap the photo credit (v2) | Opens the photographer's Unsplash page in Safari |
| A slider crosses WCAG 3, 4.5 or 7 | Light haptic tap |
| Photo changes / button pressed | Light haptic tap |
| Score or hex changes | The label rolls letter by letter to the new value |
| Reduce Motion on **(assumed)** | Rolling letters, slides, the circle toggle and grip swelling become simple fades. Haptics stay |

The app remembers nothing between launches. There's no history and no favorites.

## Avoid

These are specific to this app. Each one rules out a tempting wrong turn.

**Scope creep from Matt's app.** He has these features; we deliberately leave them out:
- APCA scoring;
- the jump-to-threshold buttons;
- the swap button;
- editable sample text;
- camera-roll or camera photos;
- copy URL, copy parameters and Markdown export;
- share links and the embed or display modes;
- keyboard shortcuts;
- the 20-font specimen rotation.

**Glass on content.** Never put Liquid Glass on the color half, the photo, the "Aa" or circle, the sliders' gradient tracks or the swatches. Only the two bars are glass.

**Re-deriving the color math.** Don't use Apple's color APIs, or a "close enough" formula, for conversions or contrast. Port Matt's engine and prove it matches his outputs. A hex that's off by one, or a ratio that rounds differently, is a bug.

**Shipping the Unsplash key in the app.** That includes an `Info.plist` entry, a hard-coded constant, a test fixture or a debug build setting. The app talks only to the owner's Vercel function.

**Calling Unsplash per frame or per swipe.** Fetch in batches as Matt does (10 at a time, refill when 3 remain), so a swipe never waits on the network and the 50/hour limit holds.

**Wrong motion axes.** Matt's phone layout moves panels vertically only. Only the photo filmstrip moves sideways.

**Shrinking touch targets to match the web.** Matt's buttons are 28 pt tall. Keep that look, but give every tappable thing at least a 44 × 44 pt touch area.

**Breaking his look with system defaults.** Avoid:
- SF Pro in the UI;
- blue tint color;
- default `Slider`;
- `NavigationStack` or titles;
- `TabView` dots;
- `List` styling.

**Distribution work.** No App Store metadata, privacy labels, TestFlight setup, iPad or landscape layouts, onboarding or analytics.

**Touching Matt's repo.** `~/Desktop/GitHub/mds-color-shift` is read-only reference: no commits, edits or `pnpm` runs there.
